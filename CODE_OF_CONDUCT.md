# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Variaxiom a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community and the public interest.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, coordinated manipulation, and conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project stewards may clarify and enforce standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code. Enforcement decisions should be documented proportionally while protecting the privacy and safety of reporters.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact route listed in `SECURITY.md`, marking the report `CODE OF CONDUCT`. Do not disclose sensitive reports publicly.

## Enforcement guidelines

Stewards use a graduated response: correction, warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal, depending on impact and pattern. Serious threats, harassment, doxxing, or deliberate security abuse may result in immediate permanent removal.
