## Path to shared maintainership

Maintainer rights are earned through sustained, reviewable work rather than follower count or affiliation. Candidates should demonstrate:

- repeated technically substantive contributions;
- reliable review and respectful community conduct;
- understanding of capability/authority separation;
- willingness to document uncertainty and regressions;
- no undisclosed conflict affecting a decision area.

No single maintainer should permanently control all of:

- kernel/security policy;
- evaluator acceptance;
- releases;
- financial sponsorship;
- community conduct enforcement.

The governance roadmap moves from founder-led stewardship to domain maintainers and then a small elected/appointed council after the project has enough independent contributors to make the structure real rather than ceremonial.
