# Variaxiom trademark policy (draft)

The source code is licensed under Apache-2.0. That license does not grant rights
to imply endorsement or to present a modified distribution as the official
Variaxiom project.

Until a formal trademark policy is adopted, you may truthfully say that a work
is "based on Variaxiom" or "compatible with Variaxiom." Do not use the name or
logo in a way likely to confuse users about the source, governance, or security
review status of a distribution.

This is a practical project policy, not legal advice. The name has received only
preliminary public namespace clearance; a professional trademark search remains
a pre-launch legal task.
