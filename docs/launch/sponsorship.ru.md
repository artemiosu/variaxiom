# Стратегия спонсорства, грантов и инвестиций

## Последовательность капитала

### Стадия 1 — credits и in-kind support

Цель: снизить стоимость экспериментов без изменения governance.

Подходящие партнёры:

- model/API providers;
- cloud/GPU providers;
- sandbox/runtime vendors;
- observability platforms;
- security tooling;
- CI/repository infrastructure.

Просить не «деньги на сингулярность», а конкретный ресурс для public benchmark/Trial с прозрачным sponsor disclosure.

### Стадия 2 — GitHub Sponsors и маленькие grants

После нескольких Trials:

- monthly sponsorship для maintenance;
- one-time funding на evaluator/reproduction;
- open-source/security/research grants;
- sponsored bounties с независимыми acceptance criteria.

### Стадия 3 — strategic sponsorship

После внешних integrations и contributor base:

- funded adapter/reference implementation;
- co-sponsored benchmark;
- security audit;
- conference workshop;
- hosted public observatory.

### Стадия 4 — institutional investment

Имеет смысл, когда есть product wedge и отделимый business layer.

Возможные коммерческие продукты вокруг открытого protocol:

- hosted lineage/evidence observatory;
- private evaluator pools and holdout management;
- enterprise policy, identity, audit, and compliance;
- managed sandbox fleet;
- agent change-control CI/CD;
- model/harness optimization service;
- certified skill/tool supply chain.

Open protocol, schemas, local runner и core lineage остаются открытыми; коммерческая ценность строится на hosting, governance, integrations, compliance и operations.

## Investor narrative

Проблема:

> Агенты начинают писать tools, сохранять skills и менять harness’ы быстрее, чем организации способны доказать безопасность и полезность этих изменений.

Решение:

> Variaxiom — CI/CD + evidence supply chain для agent evolution: immutable candidates, protected evals, authority gates, lineage и rollback.

Timing:

- self-harness research уже показывает измеримые gains;
- major agent products добавляют memory, skills, plugins, subagents и auto-review;
- чем больше self-modification, тем выше спрос на neutral proof layer.

## Evidence packet before fundraising

- reproducible public demo;
- 3+ independent reproductions;
- 2 external harness adapters;
- published benchmark or evaluator-hacking corpus;
- 10+ meaningful non-founder contributors;
- recurring users/experimenters;
- signed design partners or strong letters of interest;
- security roadmap and legal entity/IP clarity;
- documented open-source business model;
- realistic 18-month budget and milestones.

## Sponsorship ethics

- publish all material sponsor relationships;
- sponsor cannot secretly alter evaluator or suppress negative results;
- sponsored Trial includes raw evidence and limitations;
- maintain dependency diversity;
- no token/coin tied to project governance;
- no investor claim that the project guarantees AGI/singularity.

## Example small sponsor ask

> Fund 5,000 isolated candidate/evaluator runs and an independent sandbox review for Variaxiom Trial #003. Results, failures, costs, and artifacts will be public; sponsorship receives acknowledgement, not control over outcomes.
