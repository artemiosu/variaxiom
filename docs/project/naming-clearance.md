## Rationale

**Variaxiom** combines:

- `variation`: the source of evolutionary alternatives;
- `axiom`: an explicit invariant that candidates cannot bypass.

It directly encodes the architecture: nearly everything may vary, but inheritance is constrained by constitutional evidence gates.

## Communication properties

- distinctive coined word;
- pronounceable in English and Russian (`вариаксиом` / `вариаксиома` as an informal Russian rendering);
- no generic “AI,” “agent,” “GPT,” “auto,” or “evolve” suffix;
- suitable for project, protocol, CLI, and package family;
- supports a memorable tagline: **Agents mutate. Evidence decides.**
